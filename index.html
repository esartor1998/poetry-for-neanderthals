How to play

A party game for two teams and a bonk-stick. The Poet gets their team to guess a word while speaking only in words of a single syllable.

The game

  • Split into two teams and take turns. Each turn, one player is the Poet.
  • Each card has a 1 word and a harder 3 word or phrase. The Poet gets their team to guess it.
  • The catch: the Poet may only say words with one syllable.

    "Big cat. Has mane. Goes roar." works. "Enormous feline" does not.

  • Slip up with a longer word and the other team gets to (nicely) hit you, and the card costs your team −1.
  • Skipping a card also costs −1. Running out of time on a card doesn't cost anything.
  • Most points after an agreed number of rounds wins.

Using this page

  • Pick a round length and the card packs you own, then hit Start.
  • The Poet holds the screen. The other team watches over their shoulder so they know the words and can catch slips.
  • After each card, tap +1 or +3 for what the team guessed, or −1 for a bonk or a skip. The next card shows up on its own. When time runs out mid-card, just pass it on, there's no penalty.
  • On a keyboard, 1 and 3 score, and B or - is a bonk.
  • When the fuse burns out, hand it to the next team and tap Next team. The deck carries on, so nobody sees a repeat until every card's been used.
  • On a phone, the screen stays awake during a round.
